Your dashboard is the at-a-glance view of your work in Adopt — the first screen to open each day.

  ![Screenshot 2026 07 27 At 12 07 04 PM](/images/Screenshot-2026-07-27-at-12.07.04-PM.png "Screenshot 2026 07 27 At 12 07 04 PM")
</Frame>

## What it shows

- **Pending review** — items waiting on your approval. This is usually where you start your day.
- **Recent runs** — what's run lately, and whether each succeeded or failed.
- **Your usage** — a summary of your own activity.

## Using it well

- Treat **pending review** as your to-do list. Clearing it is the point.
- If **recent runs** shows failures, open one to see why — it's often a missing or unsynced file. See [When a run fails](/troubleshooting/when-a-run-fails).
- Admins get a broader dashboard across the whole org, including everyone's pending queue — see [Usage and reporting](/admin/usage-and-reporting).
