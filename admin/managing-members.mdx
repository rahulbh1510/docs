Members are the people in your org who use Adopt. As an admin, you control who they are, what role they hold, and which clients they can reach.

## Inviting a member

1. In admin settings, invite the person by email.
2. Assign a **role** (see [Roles and permissions](/security-access/roles-and-permissions)).
3. Grant access to the **clients (workstreams)** they need. Access is per client — being a member of the org doesn't reveal any client by itself.

## Changing roles or access

Update someone's role as their responsibilities change, and add or remove client access as they move between engagements. Prefer the narrowest access that fits the job.

## Deprovisioning

When someone leaves:

1. Remove them from all workstreams (revokes client access).
2. Reassign any engagements where they were a reviewer, so the HITL gate isn't left without a reviewer.
3. Disable or remove their account per your org's process.

<Tip>
  Run a **periodic access review** — quarterly or annually — of who has access to what. It's good hygiene and often a compliance requirement. Look for people with access to clients they no longer work on, and roles broader than needed.
</Tip>
