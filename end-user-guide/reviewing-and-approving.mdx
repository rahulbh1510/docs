Every agent output waits for a person before it's final. That person is you. This is where most of your time in Adopt is spent.

## The review queue

Your review queue lists work that's waiting on you, marked **action needed**. Each item shows:

- which client and task it's for
- what was prepared (e.g. "3 reconciliations ready for review")
- when it finished

Open an item to see the full prepared output.

## Reviewing an item

Inside an item you see the prepared work — the workpaper, the reconciliation set, the checklist — along with the sources it used. Because Adopt agents **cite their sources**, you can trace a figure back to the document it came from. Check the work as you would a junior preparer's: is it complete, are the numbers right, are the citations sound?

## Two decisions

- **Approve** — the work is correct. It becomes final and can be shared or delivered. Your approval is recorded with your name and the time.
- **Send back** — something's off. Add notes describing what needs to change, and the work returns for correction. Nothing is shared until it comes back and you approve it.

## Why your approval matters

Your approval is the professional sign-off. It's recorded as part of the audit trail, so there's always a clear record of who approved what, and when. This is what lets Adopt be used for regulated finance work.

<Tip>
  If you review the same kinds of work often, set up [notifications](/end-user-guide/notifications) so items come to you instead of you checking the queue. And if an item references a file that looks wrong or out of date, that's usually a files issue — see [Working with clients and files](/end-user-guide/clients-and-files).
</Tip>
