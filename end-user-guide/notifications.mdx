Notifications tell you when something needs your attention — most importantly, when an agent has prepared work and is waiting on your review.

## Channels

- **Email** — built in. Nothing to connect; you only choose how often you're notified.
- **Slack** — connect your workspace once, then choose a channel or direct message.
- **Teams** — connect your team once, then choose a channel or chat.

## Choosing how often (email)

Email supports three frequencies:

- **Real-time** — one message per event, as it happens.
- **Hourly digest** — events batched into one email each hour.
- **Daily digest** — one summary email per day, at a time you choose.

Digests are the way to cut noise if you get a lot of activity.

## Notification rules

A rule decides which agents, which events, and which channels you're notified on. The events you can choose from:

| Event | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **HITL: action needed** | An agent paused and needs your review. This is the important one, and it's on by default. |
| **Run completed** | A run finished successfully. |
| **Run failed** | A run stopped with an error. |
| **Awaiting review \> 4h** | Something has been waiting on a reviewer longer than a set time (an escalation). |

You can create more than one rule — for example, real-time Slack alerts for "action needed" on your close agents, and a daily email digest for everything else.

## Setting it up

Notifications live in **Settings → Notifications**. Connect any channels you want, create a rule (pick agents, pick triggers, pick channels), and save. There's a **Send test notification** button to confirm it's working.
